The iPhone Air is Apple's holy grail of design. What was once a small chamfered rectangle is now a device with refined, pillowed curves. In a sea of thick, 6.9" behemoths, the Air is a device that promises something new: simplicity.

At least, you *think* that's what it's promising. If you hold this device, you can't help but assign an emotional weight to its lightness. Could this device finally be *the* minimalist phone? Sadly, this is the incongruence: it feels like less, *so why isn't it?*

If your mission is to escape the trenches of Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, or any other infinite scrolling hell, then it's easy to see why the promise of a simpler phone will lead you to a simpler life. The phone cannot change your habits. The demon is yourself. 

The iPhone Air promises a lightness, but when it's in my pocket, I'm carrying an immense weight. Is it really the phone?
